# MCDM Minion Rules

Living the Legolas/ Groot Fantasy

- Remove anything but the basic attack.
- Change **HP** to be based on the CR. [[#Minion Stats by CR]]
  - If a minion takes any damage from an attack or a FAILED save, it dies.
  - If it succeeds in the save, but the (half) damage is enough to kill it, it dies.
- **Overkill Attacks**
  - **Melee Attacks**. If the damage they do is enough to get all the hitpoints, the damage gets transferred to another minion within 5ft.
    - If the leftover damage is enough to kill the second minion, it gets transferred to the third, and so on.
  - **Ranged Attacks**. If the damage they do is enough to get all the hitpoints, the projectile passes through the monster, **killing every minion in a straight line.**
- Resistances and Immunities:
  - Remove dmg resistances.
  - Keep immunities (easy math).
  - Keep saving throw proficiencies.
- Replace multiattack with a group attack:
  - The whole group makes one attack
  - Change the "To Hit" number to be (Proficiency + Number of monsters in the attack).
  - Change the damage to the one from the table [[#Minion Stats by CR]], then **Multiply by the number of minions in the attack.**
  - If it has a saving throw, make it DC (8 + Proficiency + Number of monsters in the group attack)
