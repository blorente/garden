## Kalzumeus Podcast #3 on gretting your first client

- When choosing testimonials, choose the ones that demonstrate that you have solved the problem that Your Customer Persona has.
  - E.g. a violin teacher that has a testimonial saying “thanks to the discipline of htese lessons, my kid is getting better grades”, after figuring out that most her customers were asian moms that wanted their sons to get into Harvard.

### Podcast on charging more and scaling up consulancy

- Drip marketing is mostly about establishing yourself as “the person who knows their stuff about a particular topic” in the minds of your prospects.
  - Of course, you can only do that by delivering astounding amounts of value.
- A branded email “Bardic Tools update” is less effective than an email that’s written for them “try this new cool thing coming to bardic tools”
- You can charge a percentage of the percieved value you generate.
  - Get percieved as generating more value, by generating more value, and being percieved as such.
- Being a domain expert is what will get you tons of clients at a higher rate.
- Charge more.
- About 80% of their time was spent on unbilled time (training, outreach, contracts, etc.)
  - The 20% where they finally did something was intense.
- Always phrase solutions in terms of your customer’s problems.
- Use the same language they do. If they talk about synergistic enterprise solutions, talk about those.
