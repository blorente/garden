# Make Boring Plans - Camille Fournier

Main ideas:

- Very often, we confuse vision with strategy.
  - Having a very strong vision that "we're going to migrate to the cloud and it's going to be great" is not enough, _even if_ you are indeed migrating to the cloud, and it is going to be great.
  - You need to turn that into an actionable plan.

- In platform, we're dealing with novel technology, so we're spending a lot of innovation tokens by necessity.
  - Reduce the overall innovation token spend by making boring plans ahead of time.
  - Things like Proofs of Concept, migrating a single piece to get a better sense of the whole, and learning, all help make a plan boring.

- In platform engineering specifically:
  - it's better to do the hard/interesting parts (e.g. to get `git` to be performant in monorepos) ahead of time, **before** people are clamoring for it.
    - We cannot afford to rush foundational infrastructure, so instead we delay deadlines, and that's not a good look.
  - The goal for platform engineering is **to have capacity ready just before someone needs it**. You need to do a lot of prep work for that.
    - If you're not ready with Kubernetes when a product team wants it, they're going to stand up their own, and you know how _that's_ going to end.
