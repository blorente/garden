# Oblex Tactics

- They don't need to kill the victim to drain memories. They can, but they don't have to.
- Drain memories imposes reverse guidance.
- HIGH INT! Oblexes have a keen sense of a target's weaknesses, especially after draining memories once.
- Remember that they can squeeze for free and have fire aversion.
- Prefer targets with high int and low wis.
  - Eldeth?
  - Decide if the Oblex wants to use Detect Thoughts to know the stats.
- Spells:
  - **Charm Person**
    - Victims have advantage if fighting the oblex. So will try to cast when friendly.
    - Boosted to up to 5 ppl within 30 ft.
    - Lasts for one hour.
  - **Color Spray** is for running away. It's AoE, and blinds.
  - **Detect Thoughts** is for choosing victims. Who is the highest int, the lowest wis?
  - **Hold Person**  Hold dumb, dangerous people who are not interesting.
    - is boosted, so it affects two targets.
    - Concentration
- Strategy:
  - Impersonate, try to charm your way into detecting who is smartest.
  - Then, try to isolate that target.
  - If PCs start suspecting, cast "mass charm person".
  - Eat Memories once.
  - Repeat for every interesting PC.
  - After that, Skiddaddle, maybe letting the simulacrums pool on the ground.
