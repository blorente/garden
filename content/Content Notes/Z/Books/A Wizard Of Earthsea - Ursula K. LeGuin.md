# A Wizard Of Earthsea - Ursula K. LeGuin

I just finished A Wizard of Earthsea. It was good! I think it's a bit weird, but that's fine. Weird why? Because  the main character is not really a character at all. It's a bit wooden, and the emotional beats don't hit at all, because the pacing doesn't support it.

But it is a beautiful metaphor of the battle with the Ego, the value of patience, what it really means to grow up, and what true power is.

→ Manhood is patience. Mastery is often times patience.

→ Ged becomes a super-powerful demigod in chapter 2, but that's not useful for the real fight, the one with himself.

I want to read more by LeGuin, so I guess that's a notable victory
