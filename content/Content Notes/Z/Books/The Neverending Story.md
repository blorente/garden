# The Neverending Story

A great coming of age story :D
The protagonist is a little asshole for much of the second half, but I feel like that's necessary to grow in the end.
Also, the _first_ half can feel disconnected, but that's also necessary _for the character_ of Bastian (not for the audience) to know what is possible, by seeing Atreyu.

Then he goes on to mimic what he thinks an Atreyu is, before discovering true strength.

I cried at the end.

It has a lot of really really imaginative locations and stories, even if told very simplistically and with _okay_ prose. Well, it's a children's book after all.

Favourite quote, by far, happens in the Change House:

> You see, it’s called the House of Change not only because it changes itself but also because it changes anyone who lives in it. And that was very important to the little boy, because up until then he had always wanted to be someone other than he was, but he didn’t want to change.

How many times have we wished our circumstances were different, yet have done nothing to change ourselves or the circumstances?

We have to believe we are capable of change, allowed to change, and then change.

Another banger:

> There’s no simple way of getting there.’ After a short silence she added: ‘But every way that leads there is the right one.’

And another one about having to change your self-image in order to grow, which is painful:

> And to find that picture you will have to forget the one thing you have left: yourself. And that takes hard, patient work.

Everything from the City of Old Emperors is an absolute banger and dropping knowledge bars. Well, wisdom bars.
